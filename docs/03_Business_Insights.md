# Business Insights

## 1. Revenue Insights

- The business generated revenue across multiple months, with noticeable variation in monthly performance.
- Revenue performance should be monitored over time to identify growth trends and weaker periods.
- Understanding monthly revenue patterns can help businesses plan marketing campaigns and sales strategies.

### Revenue Performance

The dataset recorded $304,815.00 in purchase revenue across 3,563 unique transactions. The average order value was $70.27.

### Business impact

The average order value provides a baseline for measuring revenue growth. Increasing average order value through product bundles, cross-selling, upselling, and free-shipping thresholds could improve revenue without requiring the same proportional increase in traffic.

### Data-quality observation

The dataset contains 4,488 purchase events but 3,563 unique transaction IDs. This difference should be investigated using the duplicate transaction-ID check before treating every purchase event as a separate completed order.

## 2. Product Insights

- Product-level analysis helps identify the products that contribute the most revenue.
- High-revenue products can be prioritized for marketing and promotional activities.
- Low-performing products may require pricing, promotion, or product-placement improvements.

## 3. Customer Insights

- Customer behavior analysis helps understand how users interact with the ecommerce website.
- Tracking purchasing users and purchase activity provides insights into customer engagement and conversion.
- Improving the customer journey can help increase the number of completed purchases.

## 4. Funnel Insights

- The purchasing funnel shows how users move from browsing products to adding products to the cart and finally completing purchases.
- A significant drop between funnel stages indicates an opportunity for conversion-rate improvement.
- Reducing friction during the checkout process may help increase completed purchases.

## 5. Traffic Source Insights

- Traffic source analysis helps identify which acquisition channels generate valuable users and revenue.
- High-performing traffic sources should receive greater attention in future marketing campaigns.
- Poor-performing sources can be reviewed and optimized to improve marketing efficiency.

## 6. Device Insights

- Revenue was analyzed across different device categories such as mobile, desktop, and tablet.
- Device-level performance helps identify how customers prefer to interact with the ecommerce platform.
- Businesses can use these insights to improve the user experience on high-value devices.

## 7. Cohort Retention Insights

The November 2020 cohort contained 79,421 first-time users. Of these users, 4,651 returned in December 2020, resulting in a month-one retention rate of 5.86%.

### What happened?

Only 5.86% of users who first visited in November returned in the next month.

### Why it matters

A low return rate may limit repeat purchases and long-term customer value. Acquiring visitors is not enough if most users do not return to continue browsing or purchasing.

### Business impact

Improving month-one retention can increase repeat engagement and create more opportunities for conversion without relying solely on new customer acquisition.

### Recommendation

Introduce retention initiatives such as personalized email campaigns, abandoned-cart reminders, product recommendations, loyalty incentives, and remarketing campaigns. Measure month-one retention after implementation to evaluate improvement.

### Limitation

The GA4 sample dataset covers November and December 2020 only. Therefore, retention can be measured for only one post-acquisition month.

## 8. Business Recommendations

### Improve Conversion Rate
Analyze the stages where users drop out of the purchasing funnel and optimize those steps.

### Focus on High-Performing Products
Promote products that generate higher revenue and evaluate underperforming products.

### Optimize Marketing Channels
Invest more in traffic sources that generate strong engagement and revenue.

### Improve Device Experience

### Monitor Revenue Trends
Track revenue regularly to identify growth opportunities and periods of weaker performance.
